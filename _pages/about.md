I am a CS PhD student at the University of Southern California, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Evi Micha](https://evi-micha.github.io/). My research interests lie broadly in [CS Theory](http://viterbi-web.usc.edu/~cstheory/), especially in areas relating to economic theory like algorithmic game theory, fair allocation, and computational social choice. As an [ORAI Fellow](https://sites.google.com/usc.edu/usc-nrt-orai/), I am funded through the NSF Research Traineeship on Operations Research and Artificial Intelligence.

During my undergrad at the University of Rochester I was lucky to be advised by [Alex Iosevich](https://alexiosevich.com/), with whom I did mathematics research in Fourier analysis.
