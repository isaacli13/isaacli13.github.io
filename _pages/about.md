I am a CS PhD student at the University of Southern California, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Evi Micha](https://evi-micha.github.io/). My research interests lie broadly in [CS Theory](http://viterbi-web.usc.edu/~cstheory/), especially in areas relating to economic theory. These include
 - **Algorithmic Game Theory:** How can we model the behavior of strategic agents? Under what conditions is it plausible that these agents act according to certain equilibria, even when such equilibria are generally difficult to compute?
 - **Computational Social Choice:** Which voting rules lead to the best outcomes? Can a decision-maker determine an optimal candidate given only ordinal information on voters' preferences?
 - **Fair Allocation:** How can items be allocated in a fair and efficient manner? What happens when these items are indivisible?
As an [ORAI Fellow](https://sites.google.com/usc.edu/usc-nrt-orai/), I am funded through the NSF Research Traineeship on Operations Research and Artificial Intelligence.

During my undergrad at the University of Rochester I was lucky to be advised by [Alex Iosevich](https://alexiosevich.com/), with whom I did mathematics research in Fourier analysis.
